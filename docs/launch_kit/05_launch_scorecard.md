Post-relaunch totals: 391 opportunities, $31.7M of pipeline, 69 wins worth $8.5M, a 31% win rate on closed deals, and 56% of losses happening at the POV stage.

## Reading it

Pipeline and wins are close to plan. POV starts are the constraint: fewer deals are reaching proof of value than planned, and most losses happen there. JAPAC certification lags the 80% target.

## Decision rules

- POV starts below 85% of plan for three straight weeks: add SE capacity or a packaged 30-day POV kit.
- A region below 70% certification at week 12: targeted enablement sprint and manager sign-off.
- Tier A share of new pipeline below 50%: tighten routing to the ICP.
