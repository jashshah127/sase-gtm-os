# Three-minute walkthrough script

Record at 1440 x 900 with the browser zoomed to 100%. Talk to one person: the hiring manager.

**0:00 to 0:20. Briefing.** "This is a working model of the SASE Product GTM Manager role, built on
synthetic data calibrated to Palo Alto Networks' public numbers. The answer comes first: in this
firewall base, the SASE opportunity is a coverage problem, not a demand problem."

**0:20 to 0:35. Market context.** "This page is the real-data layer: earnings releases, Dell'Oro, SEC
filings. Palo Alto's SASE ARR is growing about twice as fast as the market, and the market is
consolidating onto single-vendor platforms."

**0:35 to 0:50. The three findings.** Scroll the findings. "About 300 customers are missing from
territories with no specialist. SD-WAN nearly doubles win rate against Zscaler and Netskope. And Q1
looks fine, but Q2 pipeline is thin, so that's the risk."

**0:50 to 1:20. Revenue intelligence.** Open Attach and whitespace, hover a red dot. "Each dot is a
territory. The model was trained only where coverage is good, so red dots under the line are demand
we're not reaching." Set Region to EMEA to show everything recalculates.

**1:20 to 2:10. Planning simulator.** "Current capacity reaches 85% of the FY27 target. Hiring alone
would take 14 specialists." Select Load recommended plan. "The two plays plus four hires get to 100%."
Drag hiring lead time to 6 months. "If recruiting slips, this is what it costs, which is why the plays
matter more than headcount." Scroll to the territory designer.

**2:10 to 2:40. Launch and executive layer.** Show the ICP validation table. "The Prisma Browser ICP
was built from pre-launch buyers and tested on post-launch deals." Open Executive layer: "The weekly
business review drafts itself every Friday; I edit, it doesn't replace judgment."

**2:40 to 3:00. Close.** "Everything reconciles: a Python pipeline, a data-quality gate, BigQuery SQL,
and automated tests that check this app against the pipeline. The memo is one click away. Thanks for
watching."
