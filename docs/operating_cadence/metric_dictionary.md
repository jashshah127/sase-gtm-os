# Metric dictionary

One definition per metric. Every chart, memo, and SQL file in this project uses these.

| Metric | Definition | Formula | Source | Owner | Cadence | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SASE attach rate | Share of next-generation firewall accounts that own Prisma Access. | Accounts with Prisma Access / all NGFW accounts in scope | accounts | GTM Strategy | Weekly | Counts logos, not seats. Pair with expansion whitespace to see depth. |
| SASE ARR | Annualized recurring revenue from Prisma Access, Prisma SD-WAN, and Prisma Browser. | Sum of sase_arr + browser_arr for active customers | accounts (billing snapshot) | Finance | Monthly | Company SASE ARR also includes SaaS Security and DLP; this model covers the three products above. |
| Net new SASE bookings | ARR of land and expansion deals closed-won in the period. | Sum of amount where stage = Closed Won and close_date in period | opportunities | Sales Ops | Weekly | Browser bookings are tracked separately in the launch scorecard. |
| Land win rate | Share of closed first-purchase deals that were won. | Closed Won land deals / (Closed Won + Closed Lost) land deals, by close date | opportunities | GTM Strategy | Monthly | Count-based. Small segments swing; show deal counts next to rates. |
| Dollar win rate | Share of closed ARR that was won, land and expansion together. | Won ARR / closed ARR, by close date | opportunities | GTM Strategy | Monthly | Used as the pipeline-to-bookings conversion in planning math. |
| Pipeline coverage | Open pipeline closing in a quarter relative to what is still needed that quarter. | Open ARR with close_date in quarter / (quarter plan - bookings to date) | opportunities, plan | Sales Ops | Weekly | Placeholder close dates inflate far-out quarters and deflate near ones. Read current and next quarter first. |
| Quarter-to-date pacing | Bookings to date against a straight-line share of the quarter plan. | QTD bookings / (quarter plan x share of quarter elapsed) | opportunities, plan | Sales Ops | Weekly | Quarters are back-loaded; compare to the same week last year before calling a miss. |
| Pipeline created | ARR of land and expansion opportunities created in the period. | Sum of amount by created_date | opportunities | Marketing and Sales | Weekly | Flag weeks more than 1.5 standard deviations below the trailing 8-week average. |
| Stage stall rate | Open deals that have sat in their current stage longer than the stage's service level. | Deals with (as-of date - stage entry date) > SLA / open deals in stage. SLA: Technical Validation 45 days, other stages 30 | opportunities | Sales Ops | Weekly | Stage history is only as good as rep updates; audit the top 20 stalled deals by hand each month. |
| Slipped deals | Deals whose close date was pushed during the period. | Count and ARR of opportunities with last_push_date in period | opportunities (field history) | Sales Ops | Weekly | Repeated pushes (3+) are the signal; a single push is normal. |
| Median sales cycle | Typical days from creation to close for won first-purchase deals. | Median of close_date - created_date, won land deals | opportunities | GTM Strategy | Quarterly | Use the median; a few multi-year enterprise cycles distort the mean. |
| Attach propensity | Modeled probability an account would be a Prisma Access customer under full specialist coverage. | Logistic look-alike model on firmographics, incumbents, and platform footprint; trained on covered territories only | accounts_scored | GTM Strategy (analytics) | Quarterly refresh | A ranking tool, not a forecast. Region and coverage are excluded by design. |
| Land potential | Estimated first-year SASE ARR for a non-customer. | (mobile users x price per user + branches x price per site) x land share by segment | accounts, pricing assumptions | GTM Strategy | Quarterly | Illustrative pricing. Replace with list or realized pricing before use in planning. |
| Propensity-weighted whitespace | Land potential discounted by how likely each account is to buy. | Sum over non-customers of propensity x land potential | accounts_scored | GTM Strategy | Quarterly | Not a one-year number; convert with observed annual conversion before quoting as bookings. |
| Coverage gap | Customers we would expect under good coverage but do not have. | Sum of propensity - actual customers, for a group of territories | accounts_scored | GTM Strategy | Quarterly | Near zero in covered territories by construction; read it for uncovered ones. |
| Specialist productivity | Annual bookings per fully ramped SASE specialist. | Covered-territory bookings / average ramped specialist FTE (6-month linear ramp) | opportunities, specialists | Finance and GTM Strategy | Quarterly | Credits all covered-territory bookings to specialists; core sellers contribute too. |
| Prisma Browser ICP score | Points-based fit score derived from how early adopters differ from the base. | Sum of attribute points, where points = 12 x log2(lift), rounded to 5, capped at 30; tiers set by score cutoffs | accounts_scored | Product Marketing and GTM Strategy | Monthly during launch | Built on a small early-adopter set; re-derive after 100+ post-launch wins. |
| POV starts | Deals entering Technical Validation (proof of value) in the period. | Count of opportunities by pov_start_date | opportunities | Systems Engineering leadership | Weekly during launch | The best leading indicator of launch wins 6 to 8 weeks out. |
| Enablement completion | Share of field sellers certified on the launch. | Certified sellers / field headcount, by region | launch_enablement_weekly | Field Enablement | Weekly during launch | Certification is necessary, not sufficient; pair with first-meeting rates. |
